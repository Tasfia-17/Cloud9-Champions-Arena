### Core Movement
- **WASD** or **Arrow Keys** - Move your champion with precision
- **Mouse Movement** - Aim your attacks with accuracy
- **Left Click (HOLD)** - Continuous rapid-fire shooting
- **Space Bar** - Primary Champion Ability (2-4 second cooldown)
- **Shift Key** - Ultimate Champion Ability (7-12 second cooldown)

### Pro Player Tips
- 🎯 **Hold left-click** for sustained DPS output
- 🏃 **Strafe while shooting** to avoid incoming damage
- 👁️ **Use minimap constantly** for tactical awareness
- ⚡ **Combo abilities** for maximum impact
- 🌪️ **Stay ahead of the storm** - positioning is everything
- 🎪 **Use terrain wisely** - corners and edges for cover

## 🌪️ STORM SURVIVAL TACTICS

The **Cloud9 Storm System** is your greatest challenge:

### Storm Mechanics
- ✅ **Blue Circle = Safe Zone** - No damage, full mobility
- ❌ **Red Area = Danger Zone** - 25 damage per second
- 📉 **Shrinks Continuously** - Forces aggressive positioning
- 🗺️ **Minimap Tracking** - Always visible in bottom-right

### Advanced Storm Strategy
1. **Early Game (3:00-2:00):** Stay near center, collect power-ups
2. **Mid Game (2:00-1:00):** Position near storm edge for enemy ambushes
3. **Late Game (1:00-0:00):** Fight for center control, use abilities defensively
4. **Emergency Escapes:** Save movement abilities for storm emergencies
5. **Trap Enemies:** Use storm as a weapon, force enemies into danger zone
